This program attempts to generate Bhoop Raag alaps of sixteen notes each. Each note is played with particular emphasis on "vadi" or "samvadi" swar. 
The notes involved are from "aaroh" and "avroh" of raag Bhoop. After sequences are generated taking into account sixteen random notes from the "aaroh" and "avroh", 
we check that the resulting sequence contains "pakad" sequences into them. The "pakad" sequences determine the identity and the mood of a "raag".
